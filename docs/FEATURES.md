# Feature reference

## Home and inputs
Favorites can be reordered or removed without uninstalling apps. Hold OK for app actions. Only connected HDMI inputs are offered when Android exposes them. Names use the TV's data; custom labels can be removed to restore automatic naming. The gear opens TV settings, while the pencil opens Couchy settings. Manufacturer-specific shortcuts fall back where possible.

## Backgrounds and sound
Three original abstract loops are bundled: Aurora, Ocean and Ember. Import your own video from the wallpaper settings, then edit its title/category and shuffle eligibility. The wake setting chooses a shuffled background or preserves the previous one. Disabled built-in media stay installed; imported files can be deleted after confirmation. At least one background must remain enabled.

Import audio in Music. Edit title, artist, origin and wallpaper bindings; enable/disable tracks or delete imported files with confirmation. Multiple theme tracks are shuffled, then playback can continue from the wider library. Track edges fade. Music yields to external playback and releases resources when the launcher leaves the foreground. No commercial soundtrack is bundled. External-player artwork, position and controls depend on its media-session support and notification permission.

## Jellyfin
Enter your server and authorize a fresh Quick Connect code on that same server. The launcher session is separate from the official TV client's session. Reconnect from Account and connection after expiry. Continue watching and Next episodes come from server state; What to watch can draw from movies/series and favorites. Series drill down to seasons and episodes with descriptions and suitable artwork/fallbacks. Playback is handed to the installed Jellyfin TV client, not decoded by Couchy.

## News, weather and new series
News starts empty. Add up to 20 HTTPS RSS/Atom feeds with a name, color and category. Check feed terms first. The ticker shuffles eligible headlines; the panel filters by source and shows supplied summaries/images. Refresh intervals are 5/15/30/60 minutes; up to 180 articles from the last 48 hours are retained. Clearing sources removes the configured list.

Weather uses a manually selected city, daily/hourly forecasts and °C/°F units. It can be hidden completely. Refresh is normally 30 minutes on the active home screen. There is no automatic IP geolocation. See service/license attribution in the panel.

New series uses documented TVmaze schedule endpoints around today (three days before through three after), includes season-opening episodes and refreshes at most every six hours. US schedule/streaming coverage is incomplete and is not a region-specific movie-release catalog. Description, date, source, available rating and trailer search are shown in details.

## Albums and memories
Import JPEG, PNG, WebP or MP4; select albums for the photo screensaver, exclude individual items and edit captions/date/place/album. A place can open a map. The optional memory card shuffles eligible photographs; interval is 2/5/10/15 minutes and display time 8/12/20 seconds. Settings are in Photo albums → Home memories. The screensaver shows album, caption, date, time and available weather with readability backgrounds. RAW development and automatic AI caption generation are not built in.

## Games
Hold the game icon to choose Road crossing or Scooter. Both support a remote, pausing, saved local records, coins, unlocks and increasing difficulty. Road crossing includes a daily route; Scooter includes jumps, ducking, speed control, tricks and changing environments. OK opens the pause menu. Original game effects are available when background music is not playing. There is no online leaderboard or paid currency.

## Setup and diagnostics
The eight-step wizard remembers progress and can be reopened from System. Optional permissions are requested for their respective functions. Help is divided into topics. Diagnostics include network/media/source state and bounded local error history: 30 days, 400 entries and 256 KiB, plus a capped last-crash record. Settings and game progress persist across normally signed updates; clearing app data does not preserve them.
